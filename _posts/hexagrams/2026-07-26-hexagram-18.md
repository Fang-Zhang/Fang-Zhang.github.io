*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Corruption caused by neglect can be repaired through deliberate and courageous correction.

## Judgment

Success comes from confronting decay directly, with preparation before and consolidation after.

## Image

Wind under mountain symbolizes stirring stagnant habits and rebuilding public spirit.

## Notes

Name the root cause, fix it decisively, then guard against relapse.
