*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Great force is best stored, trained, and directed rather than spent impulsively.

## Judgment

Perseverance and disciplined cultivation enable success in major undertakings.

## Image

Heaven within mountain suggests contained power strengthened by study and self-renewal.

## Notes

Build capacity first, then deploy it when the channel opens.
