*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Healthy following is mutual adaptation grounded in right principles, not manipulation.

## Judgment

Great success comes when one serves correctly and followers respond willingly.

## Image

Thunder in the lake implies adapting activity to cycles, including rest and renewal.

## Notes

Follow what is worthy, and lead by first being teachable.
