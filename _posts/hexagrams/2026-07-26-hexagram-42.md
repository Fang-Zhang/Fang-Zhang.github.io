*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

When those above sacrifice for those below, vitality and trust multiply across the whole.

## Judgment

This is a favorable time for meaningful action and bold crossings.

## Image

Wind and thunder reinforce each other, mirroring self-improvement through imitation of good and removal of faults.

## Notes

Use periods of support to act decisively and improve character.
