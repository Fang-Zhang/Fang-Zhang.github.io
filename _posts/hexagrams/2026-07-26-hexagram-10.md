*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Careful conduct allows the weak to move safely among stronger powers.

## Judgment

Respectful behavior in dangerous proximity brings success without provoking harm.

## Image

Heaven above lake reflects social order sustained by proper distinctions and composure.

## Notes

When stakes are high, courtesy and self-control are strategic strengths.
