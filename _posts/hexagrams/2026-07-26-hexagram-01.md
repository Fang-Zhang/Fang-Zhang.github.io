*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Pure yang energy symbolizes initiating force, sustained momentum, and leadership that brings potential into form.

## Judgment

Great success comes from steadfastly aligning action with what is right over time.

## Image

Heaven’s ceaseless motion teaches disciplined self-strengthening and tireless refinement.

## Notes

Lead boldly, but pace your power with consistency and integrity.
