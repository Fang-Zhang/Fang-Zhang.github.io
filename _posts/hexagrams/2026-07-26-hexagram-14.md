*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Great resources are best held by a clear, modest person who serves more than self.

## Judgment

Supreme success comes when power and wealth are governed by generosity and restraint.

## Image

Fire in heaven illuminates all, urging discernment that curbs harm and advances good.

## Notes

Steward abundance responsibly and share it to keep it stable.
