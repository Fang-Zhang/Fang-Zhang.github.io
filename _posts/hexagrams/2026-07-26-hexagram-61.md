*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Open-hearted sincerity creates deep resonance that can reach even the least receptive.

## Judgment

Inner truth enables trust, major undertakings, and durable cooperation through perseverance.

## Image

Wind moving lake water reflects subtle moral influence that penetrates without force.

## Notes

Clear your motives first, and your influence travels farther.
