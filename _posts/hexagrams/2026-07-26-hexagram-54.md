*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Entering relationship from a secondary position requires tact, restraint, and realism.

## Judgment

Impulsive undertakings are unfavorable when role and timing are not fully aligned.

## Image

Thunder over lake points to transient attraction needing long-view discipline.

## Notes

Honor role boundaries and let dignity guide unequal arrangements.
