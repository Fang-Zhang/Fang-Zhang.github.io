*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Deep change is legitimate only when necessity, timing, and public trust converge.

## Judgment

Transformation succeeds when pursued with rightful intent, clarity, and persistence.

## Image

Fire and lake in tension mirror seasonal and social reordering through conflict and renewal.

## Notes

Exhaust reform options first, then change decisively with moral legitimacy.
