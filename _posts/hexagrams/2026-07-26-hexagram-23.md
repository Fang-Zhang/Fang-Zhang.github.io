*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Disintegration advances from below, weakening what stands above until collapse threatens.

## Judgment

In such a season, advance is unfavorable and wise stillness preserves strength.

## Image

A mountain resting on earth teaches that stability depends on broad support from below.

## Notes

When structures erode, protect essentials and wait out the cycle.
