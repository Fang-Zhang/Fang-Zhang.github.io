*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

When hostile forces ascend, strategic withdrawal protects strength for future use.

## Judgment

Retreat can be successful when done early, calmly, and with measured resistance.

## Image

Mountain under heaven suggests dignified distance from the unworthy without hatred.

## Notes

Step back before you are trapped, and leave with your clarity intact.
