*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Reducing one side to support another can be constructive when done sincerely and proportionately.

## Judgment

True decrease brings good fortune when simplicity replaces display and purpose stays clear.

## Image

Lake below mountain teaches curbing excess emotion and appetite to enrich higher life.

## Notes

Cut nonessentials deliberately so critical aims can be sustained.
