*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

A modest influence can restrain stronger forces temporarily through tact and patience.

## Judgment

Small, persistent measures can prepare eventual success even when major action is blocked.

## Image

Wind herding clouds without rain shows subtle shaping rather than immediate breakthrough.

## Notes

Use diplomacy and consistency when direct force is premature.
