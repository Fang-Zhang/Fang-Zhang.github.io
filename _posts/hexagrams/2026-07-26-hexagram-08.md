*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Union forms around a reliable center that attracts loyalty through character, not pressure.

## Judgment

Examine whether you have the steadiness to unite others, and join in time if you do not.

## Image

Water on earth flowing together illustrates social cohesion through shared interests and trust.

## Notes

Build community by being dependable, timely, and inclusive.
