*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Like sunrise over earth, advancement expands visibility, influence, and responsibility.

## Judgment

Progress flourishes when an enlightened superior supports capable, loyal contributors.

## Image

The rising sun calls for continuous self-clarification and moral brightening.

## Notes

Advance steadily, stay unresentful under delay, and let merit speak.
