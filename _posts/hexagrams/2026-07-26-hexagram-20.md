*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Seeing clearly and being worth seeing are twin duties of ethical leadership.

## Judgment

Sincere inward concentration inspires confidence more than outward display.

## Image

Wind over earth suggests surveying reality broadly, then shaping conduct by what is observed.

## Notes

Cultivate presence that teaches silently through example.
