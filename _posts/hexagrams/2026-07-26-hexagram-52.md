*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Stillness is mastery of motion by aligning rest and action with the right moment.

## Judgment

When the ego quiets, one can move through the world without harmful error.

## Image

Twin mountains symbolize boundary-conscious thought that stays within present reality.

## Notes

Stop internal overreach and clarity returns to decision-making.
