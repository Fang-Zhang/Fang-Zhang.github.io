*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Communities cohere around shared ritual, purpose, and a trustworthy center.

## Judgment

Gathering succeeds through perseverance, strong leadership, and meaningful common offerings.

## Image

A lake over earth warns that concentration of people and resources needs preparedness against disruption.

## Notes

Unify hearts first, then organize defenses and shared work.
