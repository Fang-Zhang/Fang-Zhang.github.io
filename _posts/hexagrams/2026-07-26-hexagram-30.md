*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Brightness depends on what it attaches to, so clarity must be rooted in right support.

## Judgment

Success comes through enduring correctness and gentle dependence that keeps light steady.

## Image

Double fire signifies cultivated illumination that can spread guidance across the world.

## Notes

Choose sustaining commitments so your insight does not burn out.
