## Overview

Cultural and spiritual refinement turns raw capacity into shared nourishment for society.

## Judgment

Great fortune comes when transformative leadership elevates people and values.

## Image

Fire over wood shows the vessel of civilization cooking substance into meaning.

## Notes

Develop talent and character so collective life is fed at a higher level.
