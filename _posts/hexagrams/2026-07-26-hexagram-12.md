*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Communication breaks between levels, allowing inferior influences to dominate public life.

## Judgment

In decline, the noble path is principled withdrawal rather than compromised participation.

## Image

Heaven and earth disconnected advise preserving inner worth when systems are corrupted.

## Notes

If integrity cannot operate effectively, step back and conserve moral capital.
