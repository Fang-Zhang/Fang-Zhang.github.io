*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Heaven and earth in harmony signal flourishing exchange between higher and lower roles.

## Judgment

When worthy forces rise and petty forces recede, prosperity and success follow.

## Image

Cosmic alignment models wise governance that balances, times, and channels abundance.

## Notes

Protect harmony by staying vigilant before complacency sets in.
