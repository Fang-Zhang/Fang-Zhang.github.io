*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Strength faces danger best by timing action, storing energy, and refusing panic.

## Judgment

Trustful waiting with inner certainty leads to success and readiness for decisive crossing.

## Image

Clouds gathering before rain suggest nourishing body and spirit while conditions ripen.

## Notes

Prepare steadily during delay so you can act cleanly when the window opens.
