*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Peak expansion brings brilliance and productivity but is inherently temporary.

## Judgment

A great leader can prosper at fullness by staying generous, clear, and untroubled.

## Image

Thunder and lightning depict swift illumination joined with decisive administration.

## Notes

At the top of success, govern carefully and prepare for change.
