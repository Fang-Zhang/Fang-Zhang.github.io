*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Rigid separations melt when shared purpose, ritual, and sincere leadership dissolve ego barriers.

## Judgment

Dispersion succeeds through steadfastness and common effort toward meaningful crossings.

## Image

Wind over water shows hardness breaking into movement and reconnection.

## Notes

When groups fragment, create shared experiences that restore common identity.
