*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Strong momentum rises, but power must remain tethered to justice and timing.

## Judgment

Perseverance is favorable only when strength is exercised with restraint and correctness.

## Image

Thunder in heaven depicts great force aligned with natural order rather than impulse.

## Notes

Use power to uphold what is right, not to prove you have power.
