*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

In unfamiliar territory, safety comes from humility, clarity, and disciplined self-possession.

## Judgment

Small success is possible for the traveler who stays correct and non-arrogant.

## Image

Fire on mountain suggests brief stations and the need for quick, fair judgments.

## Notes

Travel light in ego, heavy in principles.
