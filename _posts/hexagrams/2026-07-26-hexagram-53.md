*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Lasting advancement grows organically through patient sequence rather than leaps.

## Judgment

Good fortune follows steady maturation, like a marriage formed through proper stages.

## Image

A tree on a mountain shows influence gained over time by rooted character.

## Notes

Trust slow compounding progress and avoid shortcuts that destabilize trust.
