*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

After decline, a first returning light marks renewal beginning from within.

## Judgment

Return brings success through natural cycles, free movement, and timely direction.

## Image

Thunder within earth indicates new energy that must be protected in its infancy.

## Notes

At first signs of recovery, simplify and nurture momentum gently.
