*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

True fellowship arises from shared public purpose rather than private clique interests.

## Judgment

Open-hearted unity under principled leadership enables even difficult collective achievements.

## Image

Fire rising toward heaven suggests organized diversity rather than chaotic mixing.

## Notes

Unite around common mission, not personal loyalty networks.
