*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Collective power must be organized, disciplined, and directed by trusted authority.

## Judgment

Military action succeeds only with firm leadership, just cause, and sustained order.

## Image

Water stored in earth mirrors latent people-power mobilized through humane governance.

## Notes

Use force as a last resort and keep command clear and ethical.
