*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Difference can obstruct cooperation, yet it can also preserve needed individuality.

## Judgment

In estrangement, small careful steps can still bring good outcomes.

## Image

Fire above and lake below show coexistence without fusion, requiring principled distinctness.

## Notes

Do not force total agreement; seek workable alignment where possible.
