*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Social order begins with well-ordered roles, trust, and responsibility within the home.

## Judgment

The household prospers when enduring devotion and proper duties are maintained.

## Image

Wind from fire shows influence flowing outward from substance in word and conduct.

## Notes

Set clear expectations early and model the behavior you require.
