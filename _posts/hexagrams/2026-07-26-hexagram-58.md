*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Authentic joy comes from inner firmness expressed outwardly as openness and kindness.

## Judgment

Cheerful influence succeeds when grounded in perseverance and truth.

## Image

Joined lakes illustrate mutual enrichment through sincere exchange and shared practice.

## Notes

Choose uplifting companionship and let joy support discipline.
