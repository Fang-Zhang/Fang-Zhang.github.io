*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Uncontrived alignment with what is true enables clean action without hidden agenda.

## Judgment

Great success favors sincere rightness, while misaligned impulse leads to failure.

## Image

Thunder under heaven shows life moving naturally when guided by authentic principle.

## Notes

Act from clear conscience, not calculation, and outcomes stabilize.
