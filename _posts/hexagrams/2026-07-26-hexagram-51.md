*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Sudden shocks disrupt certainty but can awaken reverence and renewal.

## Judgment

If one keeps composure through fear, the disturbance becomes a source of success.

## Image

Repeated thunder urges self-examination and disciplined reordering after upheaval.

## Notes

Train calm before crisis so surprise does not steal your judgment.
