*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

A favorable rise of constructive influence invites responsible closeness between leaders and people.

## Judgment

Progress is auspicious now, but one must prepare for eventual reversal.

## Image

Earth above lake portrays boundless teaching and protective care from those in higher place.

## Notes

Use good timing to build trust before conditions turn.
