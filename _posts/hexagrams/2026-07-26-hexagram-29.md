*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Repeated danger tests whether one can remain true and adaptive under pressure.

## Judgment

Sincere persistence in difficulty leads to workable progress and eventual success.

## Image

Water flowing through ravines models continuity, thoroughness, and teaching by repetition.

## Notes

In crises, stay consistent, solve one hollow at a time, and keep moving.
