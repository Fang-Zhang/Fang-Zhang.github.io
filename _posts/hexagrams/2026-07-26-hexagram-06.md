*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Opposed tendencies create dispute, especially when force meets cunning or mistrust.

## Judgment

Stop short of total escalation, seek fair arbitration, and avoid risky ventures.

## Image

Heaven and water moving apart warns to prevent quarrels by clarifying terms early.

## Notes

Resolve disagreements early and preserve relationship before proving victory.
