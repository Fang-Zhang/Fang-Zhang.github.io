*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Tension releases and difficulties begin to dissolve, opening room for restoration.

## Judgment

Return quickly to normal order, and finish leftover issues without delay.

## Image

Thunder and rain suggest forgiveness, clarity, and cleansing after pressure.

## Notes

After breakthrough, simplify fast and close loose ends.
