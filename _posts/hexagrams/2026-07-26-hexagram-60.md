*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Boundaries make life workable by giving form to energy, resources, and conduct.

## Judgment

Limitation brings success, but harsh or excessive restriction eventually fails.

## Image

Water bounded by water teaches measured structure and voluntary discipline.

## Notes

Set clear limits that are firm enough to guide and humane enough to sustain.
