*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

When blocked by danger ahead and resistance behind, direct advance may fail.

## Judgment

Success comes by temporary retreat, alliance with the capable, and persistent purpose.

## Image

Water on mountain turns obstacles into occasions for self-correction and growth.

## Notes

Pause, reassess, and recruit help instead of forcing a narrow path.
