*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Beauty and refinement support order, but they must serve substance rather than replace it.

## Judgment

Grace can help in limited matters, while major issues require deeper seriousness.

## Image

Fire at a mountain’s foot illuminates form without reaching ultimate depth.

## Notes

Polish presentation, but never at the expense of core truth.
