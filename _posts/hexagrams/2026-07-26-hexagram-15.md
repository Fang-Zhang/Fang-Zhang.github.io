*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

True modesty equalizes extremes and quietly magnifies real merit.

## Judgment

Humility aligned with steady effort brings success that can be carried through.

## Image

A mountain hidden in earth models strength that does not advertise itself.

## Notes

Lower your ego, raise your standards, and your work gains lasting support.
