*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Pure yin energy represents yielding support, fertile responsiveness, and strength through devoted cooperation.

## Judgment

Good fortune comes by following rightful guidance, acting steadily, and not forcing command.

## Image

Earth’s wide capacity models patient endurance that can carry people and tasks without strain.

## Notes

Support the right direction wholeheartedly instead of competing for control.
