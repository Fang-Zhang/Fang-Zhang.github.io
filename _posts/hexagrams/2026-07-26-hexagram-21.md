*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

When obstruction blocks unity, firm adjudication is needed to restore function.

## Judgment

Success comes from just enforcement that is clear, proportional, and timely.

## Image

Thunder and lightning represent law joined with decisive execution.

## Notes

Address harmful behavior directly before it hardens into pattern.
