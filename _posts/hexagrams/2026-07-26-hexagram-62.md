*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

When small factors dominate, prudence and humility outperform grand ambition.

## Judgment

Success lies in careful correctness, handling minor matters, and avoiding overreach.

## Image

Thunder on mountain indicates heightened sensitivity requiring conscientious detail work.

## Notes

Lower your profile, tighten execution, and skip heroic moves.
