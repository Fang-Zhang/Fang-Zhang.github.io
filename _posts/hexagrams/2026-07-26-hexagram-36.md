*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

In dark times, wisdom survives by concealing brilliance without abandoning principle.

## Judgment

Adversity is navigated through inward steadfastness and outward prudence.

## Image

Light buried in earth advises quiet strength among confused or hostile surroundings.

## Notes

Protect your core values discreetly until conditions permit open action.
