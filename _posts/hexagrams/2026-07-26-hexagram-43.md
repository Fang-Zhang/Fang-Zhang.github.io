*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Accumulated tension requires decisive clearing of lingering inferior influence.

## Judgment

Declare the issue openly, avoid violent overreaction, and act with firm integrity.

## Image

A lake rising to heaven warns that accumulation must be balanced by timely distribution.

## Notes

Confront core problems transparently, starting with your own blind spots.
