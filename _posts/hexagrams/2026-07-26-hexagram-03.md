*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

New growth emerges in chaos, where beginnings are fragile yet full of latent possibility.

## Judgment

Perseverance and wise restraint bring success, especially when capable helpers are appointed.

## Image

Thunder and rain portray disorder becoming order through careful structuring effort.

## Notes

Start small, gather allies, and stabilize before pushing expansion.
