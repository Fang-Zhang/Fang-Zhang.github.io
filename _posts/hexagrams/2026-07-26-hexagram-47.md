*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Constraint by circumstances or people can compress the spirit but also test depth of character.

## Judgment

The great person can still turn hardship into eventual good through steadfast inner strength.

## Image

A drained lake portrays depletion that calls for fidelity to purpose over comfort.

## Notes

When unheard, speak less, stabilize inwardly, and endure without self-betrayal.
