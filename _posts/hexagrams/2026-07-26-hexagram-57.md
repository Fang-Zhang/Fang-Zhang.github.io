*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Subtle influence enters deeply through continuity, patience, and directional consistency.

## Judgment

Small but steady penetration succeeds, especially with guidance from the truly capable.

## Image

Following winds symbolize repeated instruction that turns intent into execution.

## Notes

Apply persistent gentle pressure in one clear direction.
