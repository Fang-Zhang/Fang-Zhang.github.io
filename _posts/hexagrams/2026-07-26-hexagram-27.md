*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

What and whom we nourish reveals our character and determines our development.

## Judgment

Good fortune comes from right nourishment of self and others through steady discipline.

## Image

Thunder beneath mountain points to moderation in speech and intake as foundations of growth.

## Notes

Feed what elevates you, and starve what weakens your judgment.
