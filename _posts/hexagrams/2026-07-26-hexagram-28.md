*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

An overloaded structure signals exceptional pressure that cannot remain as it is.

## Judgment

Success requires purposeful transition and timely action under unusual strain.

## Image

A lake above trees evokes conditions where inner steadiness must outlast external imbalance.

## Notes

When burden exceeds support, redesign quickly instead of pretending stability.
