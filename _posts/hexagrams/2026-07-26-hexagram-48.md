*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Essential human needs and shared moral resources remain constant across political change.

## Judgment

The source is reliable, but success depends on drawing from it effectively and without negligence.

## Image

Water over wood represents collective nourishment through organized mutual support.

## Notes

Maintain the system that sustains everyone, and use it properly.
