*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Collective momentum arises when movement matches the people’s spirit and timing.

## Judgment

Enthusiasm can mobilize helpers and coordinated action when guided wisely.

## Image

Thunder emerging from earth evokes emotional release that can be elevated through culture and ritual.

## Notes

Channel excitement into disciplined structure before it disperses.
