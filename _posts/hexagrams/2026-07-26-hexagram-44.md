*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

A subtle inferior force re-enters unexpectedly and can grow if indulged.

## Judgment

Do not bind yourself to seductive but destabilizing influences.

## Image

Wind under heaven signifies commands spreading widely and requiring early vigilance.

## Notes

Interrupt small harmful patterns immediately before they gain leverage.
