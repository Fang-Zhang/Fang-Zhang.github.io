*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Inexperience is natural at the start of learning, but it must be guided by discipline and humility.

## Judgment

Progress comes when the learner sincerely seeks instruction and applies it with persistence.

## Image

A spring at a mountain’s base shows character formed by thorough, stepwise development.

## Notes

Ask clearly, listen deeply, and practice fundamentals without impatience.
