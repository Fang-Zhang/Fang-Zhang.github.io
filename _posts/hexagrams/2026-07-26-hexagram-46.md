*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Steady ascent from low position succeeds through humility, effort, and patient growth.

## Judgment

Great success follows when one seeks worthy guidance and keeps active momentum.

## Image

Wood growing within earth depicts gradual elevation by accumulated small advances.

## Notes

Climb step by step and let consistency outrun ambition.
