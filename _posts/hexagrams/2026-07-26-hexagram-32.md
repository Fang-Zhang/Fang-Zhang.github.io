*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Endurance is not stasis but lawful continuity that renews itself through change.

## Judgment

Perseverance in right direction brings lasting success and meaningful progress.

## Image

Thunder and wind show movement that remains faithful to an inner guiding principle.

## Notes

Keep your core values fixed while adapting methods to conditions.
