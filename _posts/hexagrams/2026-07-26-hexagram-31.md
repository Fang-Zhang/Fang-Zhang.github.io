*By Fang Zhang*

## Overview

Mutual attraction works best when inner stillness meets outward joy with respect.

## Judgment

Influence succeeds through considerate approach, constancy, and proper timing.

## Image

A lake on a mountain teaches receptive humility as the basis of real persuasion.

## Notes

Win hearts by listening first, then speaking from grounded sincerity.
